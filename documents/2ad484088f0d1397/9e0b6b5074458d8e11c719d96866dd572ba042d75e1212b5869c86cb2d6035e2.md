Crusoe Cloud operates data centers powered by clean energy sources. Each location is selected for its access to renewable or sustainably sourced power.

## Data centers[​](https://docs.crusoecloud.com/reference/locations#data-centers "Direct link to Data centers")

| Location | ID | Energy profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iceland | `eu-iceland1-a` | 100% renewable grid (approx. 68% hydro, 32% geothermal) |
| Houston, TX | `us-southcentral1-a` | 100% renewable via RECs |
| Virginia | `us-east1-a` | 100% renewable via vPPAs, RECs, and Guarantees of Origin |
| Sparks, NV | `us-west1-a` | Partnership with Redwood Materials using second-life EV batteries for AI compute energy storage |
| Norway | `eu-norway1-a` | 100% hydro (coming online soon) |
