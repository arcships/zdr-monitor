## [How do the bots work?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01HCFXCW5CGQEZDTQRBGBJQTB7)

The bots on Poe are powered by third-party companies that use large language models (LLMs). LLMs are machine learning systems that have been trained on vast quantities of data in order to process and generate text,images, audio, video, and more.

## [What are points?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01HCFXCW5CB1DSH6F4WXXYHJFJ)

Bots consume points to give a response. Each user has a pool of points that flexibly work with every bot on Poe. Each bot displays information about the number of points required to send a message (e.g. it could be a fixed price per image created, or a variable number based on how much text it reads and writes). This means you have the freedom to choose how you allocate your points.

Throughout Poe, you will also see the approximate cost of pre-purchasing those points at a rate of $30 per 1M points.

Non-paying users receive points daily, which reset at the end of each 24-hour period. Points do not roll over. If you need more points, you can wait for them to renew tomorrow, or pay for more now.

Subscribers receive a higher number of points than non-paying users receive. They also gain access to the most cutting-edge bots on Poe. Subscribers can also purchase add-on points. See all the[benefits of subscribing](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19945140063636)in the Subscription FAQ.

## [How many points will/did my message use?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01JDJ4T29Y76DJ3HGN3X8PXJP9)

There are a few ways to evaluate how many points a message uses:

**Check the bot’s information about the number of points required to send a message.**If you select an existing chat or create a new chat, the very top has a bot description which includes an approximate first message cost and a 'Rates' button.

In ‘Rates’, you will see all available details on how point prices are calculated.

**Set a budget.**To help you budget points, we will notify you when a message or bot exceeds your budget. Then you can choose between raising the chat’s budget or not. You will never spend more than the budget on a single message unless you explicitly agree first.

*   For conversational chats: In the general Settings menu, you can set a global per-message budget for all chats. In each individual chat, you can tap the bot's name to open the chat settings, where you can set that specific chat's per-message budget.
*   For interactive canvases: Access the canvas tab 'budget' page by opening the canvas tab overflow menu (accessible by clicking on the three dots in the upper right corner of the canvas tab) and clicking on 'budget.' Here you can set a cumulative budget for this specific canvas tab and review your spend.

**Look at the points used by a past message.**

*   For a specific message, open its menu (the three dots that appear when hovering your mouse over a message, or the menu that appears when long-pressing a message on a phone). Select ‘info’ to see the point cost, including any breakout of what led to that number of points.
*   To look across all messages, go to Settings on [Poe.com](http://poe.com/) on an internet browser. In the Points section is a button “View Points History”.

## [What is auto-manage context and how does it work?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01JPT1BAZ72XMQ9N73V9C1G9KJ)

Auto-manage context is a helpful feature that balances your bot’s memory with compute point usage. When enabled, it controls how much of your chat history is sent to the bot with each new message, prioritizing recent interactions.

By keeping this feature on, you can chat for longer without using as many points, and in most cases, you won’t notice a difference in the bot’s responses. You can always turn this feature off when you need the bot to remember earlier parts of your conversation, but be aware this will increase the total points used per message.

## [What are shared subscriptions and how do points get distributed with a shared subscription?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KP91DAC7K0JM8VYJ4S6B3ZQN)

Some subscriptions may be eligible to support a shared family, friend or team subscription (“shared subscription" and each member will be a “group member”). A shared subscription enables subscribers to invite a limited number of group members to use their points, subject to Quora’s Terms of Service. Each group member’s point usage is unlimited (i.e. once a group member is added they can individually use up to 100% of the subscriber’s points). Group members must be registered Poe account holders and won’t be eligible to purchase additional points through the subscriber’s account if the subscriber’s points run out. Only the subscriber will be able to purchase additional points. The subscriber will have access to group members’ aggregate usage behavior and may remove a group member at any time. Group members will not have access to the subscriber’s or other group members’ account data. Group members are under no obligation to participate in a shared subscription and may leave the shared subscription at any time. If a group member is no longer an active member of a shared subscription or a Poe account holder, they will lose access to the subscriber’s points. If the subscriber is no longer a Poe account holder, all group members will lose access to the subscriber’s points. The terms of shared subscription are subject to change.

## [How can I make sure the bot remembers our entire conversation? Sometimes it seems to forget things.](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01JDJ4T29Y9Z1CNBNNE5SB1XT9)

First, choose a bot that can handle the type of content and size of memory you want it to remember. Every AI model powering the bots has a maximum context length. Read bot descriptions for details. If your conversation length exceeds that amount, Poe will notify you.

Second, turn off the ‘Auto-manage context’ setting in your chat thread. By turning it off, you will include all chat history up to the bot’s maximum, rather than balancing quality results with reducing your compute point usage. Note: Including everything may use up a lot more points, and often does not improve the results. To turn off ‘Auto-manage context’:

*   On your specific chat, tap the bot’s name to open the chat settings page.
*   Next to 'Auto-manage context’, set the toggle to off.

## [Can other people using Poe see my conversations with the bots?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01HCFXCW5CMZ0MWHG2EJ6TJN38)

No. Conversations with bots are private and are not visible to other users of Poe.

If you tap the Share button, you will be prompted to choose any messages you want to share with others, and the messages you select will then be copied to a new share object, which will be visible to whoever you share it with.

If you choose “Share on Poe” as part of the share flow, the messages you have chosen to share will be copied to a post that will be visible to other Poe users.

You can see all of your posts on Poe on your profile, which you can access via the left side menu (accessible from the button in the top left of the screen in the bot conversation view). You can delete any of your posts from the “...” menu, accessible in the top right of the post view.

## [How can I clear my chat history with a bot?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01HCFXCW5C8JF2R4SVV2T2WEGY)

If you want to clear your chat history, you have two options: delete the entire chat history or delete individual messages.

Deleting your entire chat history:

*   Open Poe and go to “Settings”.
*   Scroll down to find the "Delete all chats" option.
*   Tap on it, then confirm that you want to delete your entire chat history.

Deleting individual messages:

*   Open Poe and go to the chat where you want to delete messages.
*   Long-press (on the app) or right-click (on web) on one of the messages, then tap on "Delete".
*   Select one or more messages to delete. A green checkmark will appear for each message you select.
*   After selecting the message(s), tap "Delete" to remove them.

## [What is Memory?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KQ8KEG313PGGE7VGZNS8Z6XC)

Memory helps bots get to know you over time by picking up on things like your preferences, interests, and what you're working on, and sharing that context with other model providers you interact with on Poe — so you don't have to repeat yourself across chats. It's off by default. You can turn it on in **Settings → Memory**, where you can also view or permanently delete your Memory at any time.

Once enabled, Poe updates your memory once a day using messages you send going forward (it won't look back at older messages). Messages sent while Memory is off are never used to build your Memory, even if you turn it back on later. Memory works across most Official bots like Claude, GPT, and Gemini, and syncs across all your devices automatically. It does not activate in group chats, with half-shield or user-created bots, or with non-chat bots like image and video generators. Memory is designed to remember broadly useful context like communication preferences, hobbies, and professional details, while skipping sensitive information.

Memory may use slightly more points per message since Memory is included as context, but the difference is typically not significant. Your Memory is never shared with other users or bot creators, and is permanently deleted if you delete your account.

## [What is a temporary chat?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KWF2X97P8WZBT7HZR2ZR2ZN4)

A temporary chat is a private, one-off conversation that isn't saved to your chat history. Use it for quick questions you don't need to keep.

When you're in a temporary chat:

*   It won't appear in your chat history, and it can't be saved.
*   It won't be saved to or used by Memory, so it won't affect your personalized experience.
*   It isn't searchable, and it can't be shared or branched.
*   It's only available with Poe's official bots.

Temporary chats still use compute points like a normal chat, and those charges appear on your Activity page (labeled "Temporary chat").

Temporary chat messages are permanently deleted within 24 hours of creation. Files you upload to a temporary chat are retained for up to 15 days for safety review before being permanently deleted. In limited cases, we may keep content longer to meet our legal and safety obligations.

## [How can I create a bot on Poe?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01HCFXCW5C8AXFR7CGC1MRXY5R)

There are two types of bots you can create on Poe: Prompt bots and API bots.

**Prompt bots** allow you to create a chatbot on top of GPT-3.5-Turbo or Claude-instant by providing plain-text instructions that the bot will stick to during conversation with users.

**API bots** are aimed at more tech-savvy developers who want to run custom code in response to every user message. API bots are more work to set up but offer a lot more flexibility in terms of what you can achieve with them.

Here's how to create each type of bot:

*   To create a prompt bot, open a web browser and go to [https://poe.com/create_bot](https://poe.com/create_bot). Alternatively, click the “Create a bot” button in the sidebar. To see more detailed steps, see [How to create a prompt bot](https://developer.poe.com/prompt-bots/how-to-create-a-prompt-bot) and [Best practices for prompts](https://developer.poe.com/prompt-bots/best-practices-for-prompts) in our [Poe for Developers GitBook](https://developer.poe.com/overview/readme).
*   To create an API bot, which requires an LLM or code that uses one, visit [https://poe.com/create_bot?api=1](https://poe.com/create_bot?api=1). For more information on API bots, check out [https://developer.poe.com/api-bots/quick-start](https://developer.poe.com/api-bots/quick-start).

Once you've created your bot, you can customize it further by clicking on the Bot name at the top, then clicking on “Edit bot” from the menu.

## [Can I create a private bot?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01HCFXCW5DZBYJ24H99ADCWF3E)

Any custom bot you create on Poe can be set to private, which means it will only be available to you and won't be displayed on your profile or be searchable by other users.

Here's how to set your bot to private:

*   Open the bot, then click on the bot's name at the top of the chat window.
*   Select "Edit bot" from the menu that appears.
*   Toggle the switch next to "Bot publicly accessible" to turn it off.
*   Click "Save" to save your changes.

Your bot is now set to private and will only be visible to you. If you want to make your bot public again, simply go back to the "Edit bot" menu and toggle the "Bot publicly accessible" switch on.

## [What are chat history cache discounts?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01JMDGNARXH8B0MN452N6N7XHP)

When a bot offers cache discounts, you will automatically get a points discount on input you send with a message when the re-sent input is big enough, you send messages quickly (generally within 5 minutes of each other but it varies) and there are no changes to previous messages. This happens when the bot can reuse parts of your previous conversation instead of processing everything again from scratch. Exact details generally differ by bot provider and model.

To see if a bot has a cache discount, find the usual message cost at the top of your chat with a specific bot, click on the spot for additional details, and read the detailed description. Common discount rates are 50% for some applicable OpenAI models and 90% for applicable Anthropic models.

## [How do I find and manage invite links to group chats?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KA9E1CSYD46QJRR6S77HD8AA)

Each chat has a unique invite link, which you can find in the **chat settings** by tapping the chat name at the top-left. All members have access to this link.

Chat admins can reset the link anytime by tapping on the **Reset link** button in the chat settings. This generates a new link and immediately invalidates the previous one.

Note: Anyone with the link can join and see the entire conversation. We recommend resetting the link if it's shared more widely than intended.

## [What chat settings and controls are available?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KA9E1CSZ9YKZXMQ788147WC9)

The chat creator becomes the **Admin** and has exclusive access to:

*   Remove users from the chat
*   Delete the chat
*   Change default bot settings and automatic replies
*   Reset invite links
*   Delete any member's messages (this deletes them for everyone)

All members can:

*   View all members and AI models in the chat
*   Leave the chat
*   Turn notifications on or off
*   Change auto-manage context settings
*   Set context clearance
*   Delete their own messages (this deletes them for everyone)

Access these settings by tapping the chat name at the top-left.

## [How does bot messaging work in group chat?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KA9E1CT1JMTF98TSNJHEM412)

The member who triggers an AI model response uses their own points. See "What are points?" and “How many points will/did my message use?” in our FAQ for details on how points are calculated. Human-to-human messages that don't trigger bot responses don't use points.

By default, bots don't automatically respond to every message in group chats. You need to mention the bot directly to trigger a response. You can change this in **chat settings** under "Automatic Replies."

## [How do I manage notification settings?](https://help.poe.com/hc/en-us/articles/19944206309524-Poe-FAQs#h_01KA9E1CT2B29AB0DJSCH05CMN)

**Note**: Your device settings override Poe settings. If you've disabled notifications for Poe in your device settings, changes made within Poe won't take effect.

By default, you receive push notifications on iOS and Android, and sound notifications on web for all new messages in all chats (including non-group chats). For example, you'll get notified when a bot completes a time-consuming task you requested.

To mute notifications for a specific chat:

1.   Open the chat.
2.   Tap the chat name at the top-left to open settings.
3.   Turn on "Mute notifications."

To unmute, open the muted chat, toggle off "Mute notifications."

On web, you can also mute/unmute by clicking the "..." menu on the chat card in the left sidebar or chat history page.
