Chat and file retention in ChatGPT … Chats: Regular and archived chats remain saved to your account until you delete them or an applicable workspace retention policy removes them. When you delete a saved chat: ChatGPT removes it from your account view immediately. OpenAI schedules it for permanent deletion from its systems within 30 days, unless the chat was already de-identified and disassociated from your account or OpenAI must retain it longer for security or legal obligations. Temporary chats don't appear in your chat history unless you save them. OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety purposes.
