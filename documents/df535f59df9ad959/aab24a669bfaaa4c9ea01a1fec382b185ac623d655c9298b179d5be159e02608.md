Services for individuals, such as ChatGPT and Codex. When you use our services for individuals such as ChatGPT and Codex, we may use your content to train our models. To opt out of model training, you can turn off "Improve the model for everyone" in ChatGPT under Settings → Data Controls, or select "Do not train on my content" in our privacy portal. For ChatGPT and Codex tasks, either option is sufficient. … Even if you have opted out of training, you can still choose to provide feedback… If you choose to provide feedback, the entire conversation associated with that feedback may be used to train our models. … Codex has separate controls for allowing training on full environments, which you can manage in the Codex Settings. Services for businesses, such as ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, and our API Platform: By default, we do not train on any inputs or outputs from our products for business users.
