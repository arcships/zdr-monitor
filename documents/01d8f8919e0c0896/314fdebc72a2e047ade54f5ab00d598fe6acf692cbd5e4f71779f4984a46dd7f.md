# Sub-processors 

Version dated 16 October 2025 

This document identifies the sub-processors that OVH uses in accordance with the provisions of the Annex “ Data Processing Agreement or "DPA"”, to carry out the processing of the Client's data as part of the Service provision. These sub-processors may be (A) Affiliates belonging to the OVH Group, hereinafter referred to as “Intra-Group Processors” or (B) entities not part of the OVH Group, referred to as “ Third-party processors”. 

As Data Controller, the conditions under which OVHcloud processes personal data relating to the Client (including its employees) are set out under OVHcloud’s “ Personal Data Usage Policy ”.
