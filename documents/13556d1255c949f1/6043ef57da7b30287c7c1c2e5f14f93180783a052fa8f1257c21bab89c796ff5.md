## Databricks Subprocessors

Databricks engages certain subprocessors in connection with providing the Platform Services or Support Services. A subprocessor is a Databricks affiliate (“Databricks Affiliate'') or a third-party engaged by Databricks, Inc. or a Databricks Affiliate to process personal data on behalf of Databricks’ customers. Capitalized terms used herein have the same meaning as defined in the [Master Cloud Services Agreement](https://www.databricks.com/legal/mcsa) or other written agreement you have with Databricks (“Agreement”).

[Subscribe to be notified](https://www.databricks.com/legal/legal-subscription) when we add new Subprocessors.
