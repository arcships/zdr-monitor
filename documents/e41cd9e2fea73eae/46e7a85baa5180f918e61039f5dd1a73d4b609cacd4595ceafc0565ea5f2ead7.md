Do you use my API inputs and outputs to train your models?

No. By default, we do not use any data submitted through our API — including prompts, inputs, outputs, or conversation histories — to train or improve our models. This commitment is contractually binding under our Terms of Service, and is further documented in our Privacy Policy. For EU/EEA users, this practice aligns with GDPR Article 5(1)(e) (storage limitation principle). Upon request, we can provide written confirmation that no User Content is retained post-processing or used for model training purposes.

Who owns the inputs and outputs submitted via the API?

You retain full ownership of all inputs you submit and all outputs you receive through our API. We only retain the rights necessary to deliver the service, comply with applicable law, and enforce our Terms of Service. Any content you generate using our API remains your intellectual property.

What data do you collect, and for how long is it retained?

We collect the following categories of data: • API inputs & outputs: Processed transiently for inference only. Not retained beyond the time required to generate and deliver Output, except where required by applicable law. Zero Data Retention is the default for all endpoints, including serverless API calls. • Technical metadata (request timestamps, error codes, usage metrics): Retained for 6 months for the purposes of billing, debugging, and security auditing. • Account & authentication data: Retained for the duration of the account plus any statutory retention periods, for the purposes of service delivery and legal compliance. Our no-retention commitment applies to the substantive content of your API inputs and outputs across all endpoints, including serverless API calls. Technical metadata for operational purposes (e.g., request timestamps, error codes, usage metrics) is handled as described in our Privacy Policy and applicable Data Processing Agreement. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting support@novita.ai.

Do you share my data with third parties?

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to or with third parties. Your data is used only to: • Deliver the API services you request • Ensure platform security and detect abuse • Comply with applicable legal obligations We may engage sub-processors to provide certain technical functions (see Q12: Sub-processors). All sub-processors are contractually bound to equivalent data protection obligations.

Can I use your API without behavioral tracking?

Yes. Our API can be used without behavioral profiling. We do not derive behavioral profiles from your inputs or outputs, nor do we share semantic analysis, pattern detection, preference inference, or other analytical outputs derived from your API usage with any third party. Technical metadata (see Q3) is used solely for operational purposes (billing, debugging, security) and does not constitute behavioral profiling.

How do you handle cross-border data transfers?

For transfers involving EU/EEA data subjects, we rely on: • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — European Commission Decision 2021/914 (Module 2: Controller-to-Processor), as appropriate to your deployment model For transfers involving UK data subjects (following Brexit), we provide: • UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) and/or UK Addendum to the EU SCCs as applicable We do not currently rely on Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) for international data transfers. We recommend consulting your legal counsel to confirm the appropriate transfer mechanism and supplementary measures for your specific use case.

Yes. We offer a DPA for enterprise customers. Our DPA covers the required elements under GDPR Article 28, including: • Subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of processing • Types of personal data and categories of data subjects • Controller's obligations and processor's obligations • Sub-processor authorization requirements • Data subject rights mechanisms • Audit and inspection rights A copy of our standard DPA template is available upon request. Execution may be coordinated through your account manager.

How do you handle content moderation and NSFW content?

Our platform applies content safety filters across applicable model endpoints. Key points: • Default settings: NSFW/content safety filters are enabled by default on most endpoints. • Per-model configuration: Filter levels vary by model. Some models (e.g., certain open-source integrations) offer configurable filter settings at the model level; contact your account manager for details. • Error handling: Content policy violations return structured error codes (e.g., InputImageSensitiveContentDetected.PolicyViolation). HTTP status codes for blocked content are standardized to 403 Forbidden. • Transparency: We recommend reviewing each model's documentation for specific safety capabilities and limitations before deployment. We do not offer customer-level (per-user/per-account) content filter controls on all endpoints. For enterprise use cases with specific content governance requirements, contact us to discuss available options.

How can I exercise my data subject rights?

If you are an EU/UK resident or otherwise entitled to data protection rights under applicable law, you may: • Access: Request a copy of your personal data • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format • Object: Object to certain processing activities To exercise these rights, contact support@novita.ai. We will respond to verifiable requests within 30 days in accordance with applicable law.
