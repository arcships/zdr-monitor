Data Controls FAQ … How do I turn off training for Codex tasks? If you use Codex on a personal ChatGPT plan, the "Improve the model for everyone" setting also applies to your Codex tasks. Turn it off in ChatGPT under Settings → Data Controls, or select "Do not train on my content" in the privacy portal. Once you opt out, new ChatGPT conversations and Codex tasks won't be used to train our models. Codex has a separate setting for training on full environments in Codex Settings. Changing the ChatGPT setting or opting out through the privacy portal does not change that Codex setting. What about Temporary Chats? Temporary Chats are deleted from our systems after 30 days. These chats: Aren't used to train our models / May be reviewed only to monitor for abuse. [注：全文无 ZDR 表述]
