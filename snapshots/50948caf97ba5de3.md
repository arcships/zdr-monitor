# Organization Plans
Compare Factory Teams, Business, and Enterprise plans for shared usage, governance, security, and support.
Business and Enterprise plans are for teams and companies that need shared usage, administrative controls, onboarding, security terms, and support. Business and Enterprise use custom limits and contract terms rather than the Individual rolling Rate Limit model.
Teams includes everything in Pro for your whole team, with up to 10 seats and centralized billing. Its price is listed with the other self-serve plans on Individual Plans.

For growing teams that need tailored plans.
Tailored for your team

- Dedicated onboarding and support

- SAML/SCIM provisioning
- Zero Data Retention (ZDR)

## Usage and billing
On Teams plans, each seat includes Pro Rate Limits. Billing is centralized and paid month-to-month.
On Business and Enterprise plans, usage is shared across the workspace and governed by contract terms. It is not governed by the Individual plan Rate Limit model.
## Enterprise Controls
Business and Enterprise add controls for platform teams:
- Identity and Access for roles, SSO, and provisioning.
- Hierarchical Settings and Org Control for inherited settings and org-managed policy.
- Network and Deployment for deployment choices and network boundaries.
- Telemetry & Analytics for usage, adoption, and cost reporting.
## FAQ
<FAQ>
<FAQItem title="Are organization plan limits per-user or shared?">
On the Teams plan, each seat has its own individual Pro Rate Limits.
Business and Enterprise plans have custom workspace-level terms. Limits, seats, support, and deployment options are scoped to the organization contract.
</FAQItem>
<FAQItem title="When should we choose enterprise instead of business?">
Choose Enterprise when you need unlimited seats, dedicated compute, partitioned inference, on-premise deployment, sub-organizations, data residency, or priority support commitments.
</FAQItem>
</FAQ>
