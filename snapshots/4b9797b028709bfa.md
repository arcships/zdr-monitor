# Manage training data and privacy
Copy pageCopy page
Control whether your data is used to train Lovable’s AI models, and understand how Lovable handles your personal data.
Copy pageCopy page
This page explains how Lovable may use your data for AI model training and how you can opt out. **Customer data** means the content you submit to or create with Lovable: prompts (including images and files you attach), code, project files, hosted applications, configurations, and generated outputs. It does not include the data your apps’ end users submit, which stays in your project’s own database and storage. As of **September 9, 2026**, customer data from **Free and Pro plans** may be used to train, develop, and improve Lovable’s AI models and AI-powered features, as described in Section 5 of the updated Privacy Policy. See the Summary of Changes for what’s changing and when. You can opt out at any time, before training begins or after. Lovable does not use the following for model training:
- **Your apps’ end-user data**: information your apps’ visitors or customers submit stays in your project’s own database and storage, and is not used to train our models.
- **Your account and billing details**: we use them to run your account.
- **Business and Enterprise workspace data**, which is excluded from training by default and remains governed by your organization’s Data Processing Agreement and contract terms.
Data opt-out
How your customer data is used depends on your plan:
- **Free and Pro:** individual users can opt their own data out of AI model training at any time with an **account-level** setting.
- **Business and Enterprise:** workspace data is governed by your organization’s agreement with Lovable, including your Data Processing Agreement, and is **excluded from model training by default**. No opt-out is needed.
Account-level opt-out (Free and Pro)
If you’re on the **Free or Pro** plan, you can opt your own data out of AI model training. Open **Account settings → AI model training** and disable the **Use my Lovable content for model training** toggle. You can opt out at any time, and your data is excluded from training going forward. Opting out is free and does not affect your use of AI features. The setting is on your account and covers the content you contribute. It does not change the setting of other members in a shared project or workspace. Each person controls their own.
If every workspace you belong to is on a Business or Enterprise plan, the toggle appears already disabled and can’t be changed: your workspace data is excluded from training by default.
If you can’t log in to your account, you can exercise your privacy choices by emailing privacy@lovable.dev.
Business and Enterprise: excluded by default
Business and Enterprise workspace data is **excluded from AI model training by default**. It is handled under your organization’s Data Processing Agreement and contract terms, which govern how workspace data is processed. Questions about your organization’s agreement: privacy@lovable.dev.
How Lovable handles personal data
- **Your account and billing details are not used for model training.** We use them to run your account.
- **We do not share the contents of your projects** (prompts, code, or your apps’ end-user data) **with advertising platforms**, unless you instruct us to do so.
- As of September 9, 2026, we may share limited **pseudonymized identifiers** with advertising platforms, as described in Section 6 of the updated Privacy Policy. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Brazil this happens only with your consent; in the United States you can opt out at any time via the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link in our website footer, your privacy settings, or a Global Privacy Control signal.
- We may create **de-identified data**, meaning data that no longer identifies you and cannot reasonably be linked to you, and use it for purposes such as improving the Services. We maintain it in de-identified form and do not attempt to re-identify it, except to test that our de-identification works.
For the complete picture, see the Privacy Policy and the Summary of Changes that took effect September 9, 2026.
FAQ
Does Lovable use my data to train AI models?
As of September 9, 2026, Lovable may use customer data from Free and Pro plans (prompts including images and files you attach, code, project files, generated outputs, and usage data) to train, develop, and improve its AI models, as described in Section 5 of the updated Privacy Policy, unless you opt out. Your apps’ end-user data and your account and billing details are not used for training, and Business and Enterprise workspace data is excluded from training by default.
- **Free and Pro:** exclude your own data by disabling **Use my Lovable content for model training** in **Account settings → AI model training**.
- **Business and Enterprise:** workspace data is excluded by default.
How do I opt out of AI model training in Lovable?
If you’re on a Free or Pro plan, open **Account settings → AI model training** and disable **Use my Lovable content for model training**. You can do this at any time, and your data is excluded from training going forward. It’s free and doesn’t affect your use of AI features.If you’re on the Business or Enterprise plan, your workspace data is excluded from training by default.
Does opting out remove my data from models that were already trained?
Opting out takes effect going forward: your content is excluded from all training data assembled after your opt-out takes effect. It does not retract content from training datasets assembled, or models trained, before then.
Is the AI model training setting available on all plans?
The account-level setting lives in **Account settings → AI model training** for every account. Free and Pro users can enable or disable **Use my Lovable content for model training** at any time, at no cost, without losing any features.If every workspace you belong to is on a Business or Enterprise plan, the setting shows as disabled and can’t be changed, because your workspace data is already excluded from training by default.
