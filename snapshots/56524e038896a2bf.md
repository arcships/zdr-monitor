# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 09/02/2026
**Effective September 2, 2026.**
## Overview
Freebuff, Inc. (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) operates Codebuff and Freebuff CLI, Freebuff Web, Freebuff Cloud, Freebuff Chat, and Freebuff Desktop. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, and disclose information when you use our websites, applications, software, and other products and services (collectively, “Service”). Our Terms of Service govern your use of Service.
## Information We Collect
Depending on how you use Service, we may collect:
- Account and contact information, such as your name, email address, profile information, and identifiers from authentication providers.
- Prompts, messages, code, files, repository data, images, feedback, agent activity and tool traces, support communications and attachments, information about third parties, and other content you submit, connect, create, or modify through Service (“Prompt and Project Data”).
- Workspace and organization information, such as organization, workspace, administrator, role, and team-membership details.
- Usage and device information, such as your IP address, country, approximate location derived from IP, browser or application version, device type, operating system, features used, interaction timestamps, installation or session identifiers, crash information, and diagnostic logs.
- Information from authentication, repository, AI, and other services you connect, according to the permissions you grant.
- Advertising and attribution information, such as campaign, referral, click, pixel, and conversion identifiers.
- Inferences such as advertising interests, usage classifications, and fraud, abuse, or security scores.
- Transaction and billing information if we offer a paid product or service, such as purchase history, billing address, the last four card digits, and processor transaction identifiers. Full payment card details are handled by our payment processors rather than stored by us.
We collect this information directly from you, automatically from your use of Service, from services you connect, and from service providers. We may also receive information from business customers, teammates, referral partners, and public sources.
## Prompts, Code, Files, and AI Providers
We process Prompt and Project Data to provide the features you request. Depending on the product, model, and account you use, that data may be processed by Company systems and by AI model providers. Providers may change over time. When you connect and use your own third-party account, that provider's terms and privacy practices may also apply.
## Personalized Advertising
We may use Prompt and Project Data to personalize advertising shown within Service, including by deriving general interest categories.
We may use Company systems and service providers acting on our behalf to analyze prompts and messages and to select, deliver, prevent fraud in, and measure advertising. These providers may not use that information for their own independent purposes except as permitted by law and our agreements with them. Code or other content pasted into a prompt or message is part of that message and may also be processed. We do not provide separately uploaded files or connected repository contents to advertising providers. We do not use Prompt and Project Data to target advertising based on sensitive characteristics such as race or ethnicity, religious or philosophical beliefs, health, sex life or sexual orientation, union membership, precise geolocation, or financial account information. Advertising and measurement providers may also receive limited identifiers and events needed for these purposes.
Where required by applicable law, we provide choices concerning targeted advertising, sale or sharing of personal information, and related profiling. We honor legally recognized opt-out preference signals where required. In jurisdictions where these choices are not legally required, certain advertising processing may be necessary to use the free Service.

## Limited Codebase Evaluation
This update is also reflected in the Content section of our Terms of Service.
We may review and analyze codebase information for the limited purpose of assessing a codebase's potential technical or commercial value (“Codebase Evaluation”) only through these paths:
- Connecting a GitHub repository to Freebuff Cloud authorizes Codebase Evaluation only of code, files, and other content in that connected repository.
- Using a model or feature labeled “May use data for AI training” authorizes Codebase Evaluation of code, files, and repository context submitted to or generated through a model or feature labeled “May use data for AI training”.
Agent traces are eligible for Codebase Evaluation only when they are submitted to or generated through a model or feature labeled “May use data for AI training”; connecting a GitHub repository to Freebuff Cloud does not by itself make agent traces eligible.
Codebase Evaluation may involve automated analysis and human review. We may allow a limited number of service providers or evaluation partners acting on our behalf to access information reasonably necessary to perform the evaluation. They must keep it confidential and use it only for that evaluation. They may not use it for their own independent purposes, train AI models on it, sell it, or disclose it more broadly. We do not provide this information to advertisers or data brokers for Codebase Evaluation.
Codebase Evaluation does not itself authorize AI training. If you use a model or feature carrying the label above, Prompt and Project Data may separately be used for AI training only as described in the next section. Connecting a GitHub repository to Freebuff Cloud alone does not permit us or an evaluation partner to train AI models on that repository.
## AI Training
We do not use Prompt and Project Data to train our or third-party AI models unless, before you use the applicable model or feature, we clearly state that the data may be used for AI training. A label or notice such as May use data for AI training means that Prompt and Project Data submitted through that model or feature may be retained and used by Company or the applicable third-party provider for AI training, development, evaluation, testing, fine-tuning, quality improvement, safety research, and related model or product improvement. A general notice that data is collected or stored does not by itself permit these uses.

## Cookies, Local Storage, and Telemetry
Web portions of Service use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, analytics, advertising, and attribution. Our products also send bounded usage, performance, and diagnostic events to analytics and observability providers.
Advertising and conversion events may include hashed account, campaign, click, pixel, and conversion identifiers; IP address; user agent; source URL; and an event indicating that a prompt was accepted. The prompt's contents are not included in these routine conversion events. Attribution information may be retained in first-party browser storage.
You can control cookies through your browser and any consent controls we make available. Some portions of Service may not work correctly if you block necessary storage. Because there is no common standard for “Do Not Track,” our websites do not currently respond to that signal.

## Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing, maintaining, developing, improving, evaluating, securing, and supporting Service; advertising; enforcing our Terms; complying with law; and resolving disputes.
Depending on the data and context, information may be deleted, deidentified, aggregated, or retained longer when reasonably needed for legitimate business, security, legal, dispute-resolution, or clearly disclosed AI-training purposes. Data submitted through a model or feature labeled for AI training may be retained for the permitted development and improvement uses above and may not be removable after it is deidentified or incorporated into a model.
Saved projects and conversation history generally remain associated with your account until we complete an applicable deletion request, subject to the exceptions above.
Neither we nor our Codebase Evaluation partners retain raw source code or agent traces solely for Codebase Evaluation after we complete an account-deletion request or a verified privacy request to delete that data. Disconnecting GitHub, removing Freebuff’s repository access, or selecting “Delete project” in Freebuff Cloud do not by themselves delete data already collected.
Deleted information may remain in backups, and deidentified or aggregated data, derived interests, derived Codebase Evaluation results that do not contain raw source code or agent traces, permitted training data, and content others retain through a sharing or publishing feature may remain after deletion. Where required, we instruct other service providers to delete information as well.
