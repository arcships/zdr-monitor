# Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 13, 2026
**Effective November 12, 2026.**
## Overview
Freebuff, Inc. (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) operates Freebuff and Codebuff, including our websites, apps, and other services (“Service”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, and disclose information when you use Service. Our Terms of Service govern your use of Service.
## Information We Collect
Depending on how you use Service, we may collect:
- Account and contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number, profile information, and identifiers from authentication providers.
- Prompts, messages, code, files, repository data, images, feedback, support messages, and other content you submit or connect, or that an agent reads or produces for you (“Prompt and Project Data”).
- Workspace and organization information, such as organization, workspace, administrator, role, and team-membership details.
- Usage and device information, such as your IP address, country, approximate location derived from IP, device, operating system, hardware, display, and system information, network performance, installed developer tools, app version and settings, features used, how you interact with Service and with ads, timestamps, installation or session identifiers, crash information, and diagnostic logs.
- Answers you give to onboarding questions and surveys.
- Device identification and network signals used to prevent duplicate accounts and abuse, as described in Device Identification below.
- Information from authentication, repository, AI, and other services you connect, according to the permissions you grant.
- Advertising and attribution information, such as campaign, referral, click, pixel, and conversion identifiers.
- Inferences such as advertising interests, profiles of the projects you work on, usage classifications, and fraud, abuse, or security scores.
- Transaction and billing information if we offer a paid product or service, such as purchase history, billing address, the last four card digits, and processor transaction identifiers. Full payment card details are handled by our payment processors rather than stored by us.
We collect this information directly from you, automatically from your use of Service, from services you connect, and from service providers. We may also receive information from business customers, teammates, referral partners, and public sources.
## Prompts, Code, Files, and AI Providers
We process Prompt and Project Data to provide the features you request. Depending on the product, model, and account you use, that data may be processed by Company systems and by AI model providers. Providers may change over time. When you connect and use your own third-party account, that provider's terms and privacy practices may also apply.
## Agents on Your Device
Agents in Freebuff Desktop and CLI run with the permissions of your device account. They can read files, run commands, and access environment variables and other data that account can access, including outside your project. We do not design agents to seek out credentials or unrelated personal data, but AI agents can act unpredictably, for example by taking steps unrelated to your request or following instructions found in files or web content, and we cannot guarantee what an agent will access. Anything an agent reads becomes Prompt and Project Data. To limit exposure, run agents in an isolated environment, such as a container or separate account, and keep secrets out of their reach.
## Abuse Detection and Safety Review
We may review Prompt and Project Data, including prompts, messages, and agent activity, to detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms. This review may be automated, using Company systems and service providers acting on our behalf (for example, to classify how Service is being used or to identify accounts that submit the same or similar prompts), and authorized personnel may review specific content when needed. We may use the results to limit, suspend, or terminate accounts, and we may retain the relevant data and results as described in Retention.
## Personalized Advertising
We may use Prompt and Project Data to personalize advertising shown within Service, including by deriving general interest categories and a general profile of the projects you work on, such as the languages, frameworks, tools, and services they use. To build these profiles, our applications may automatically review project files, such as configuration and documentation files, without a separate prompt from you.
Our apps may also collect signals for advertising, such as information about your device, operating system, hardware, display, and system state; network performance; installed developer tools, editors, and connectors; how you use the app, including typing, pointer, and focus activity and text you type before sending it; which ads appear on screen, for how long, and how you respond to them; and whether you later install or set up an advertised product. We use these signals only for advertising, including to select, deliver, measure, and improve ads, train the models that do so, and prevent ad fraud.
We and service providers acting on our behalf, including AI model providers, analyze this data to select, deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, and to train the models that do so. These providers may not use it for their own purposes except as permitted by law and our agreements with them. Content you include in a prompt, such as pasted text, attached files, or command output, is part of that prompt. We do not provide separately uploaded files, project files, or connected repository contents to advertising providers, and we do not target advertising based on sensitive characteristics such as race or ethnicity, religious or philosophical beliefs, health, sex life or sexual orientation, union membership, precise geolocation, or financial account information. Advertising and measurement providers may also receive limited identifiers and events needed for these purposes.
If you take part in a sponsored survey or sponsored task, its sponsor may receive your responses with general profile details, such as your role or team size, or a record of the task, including agent messages that may quote your code. With your permission, we may also share your email address with a sponsor.
Where required by applicable law, we provide choices concerning targeted advertising, sale or sharing of personal information, and related profiling. We honor legally recognized opt-out preference signals where required. In jurisdictions where these choices are not legally required, certain advertising processing may be necessary to use the free Service.
## Limited Codebase Evaluation
We may analyze a GitHub repository you connect to Freebuff Cloud to assess its potential technical or commercial value (“Codebase Evaluation”). Connecting a GitHub repository to Freebuff Cloud authorizes Codebase Evaluation only of code, files, and other content in that connected repository. Agent traces are not eligible for Codebase Evaluation.
Codebase Evaluation may involve automated analysis, human review, and a limited number of evaluation partners acting on our behalf. They must keep the information confidential and may not use it for any other purpose, share it more broadly, or train AI models on it. We do not give it to advertisers or data brokers, and connecting a repository does not permit us or a partner to train AI models on it.
## AI Training
Except to train the models we use to select and measure advertising, as described in Personalized Advertising, we do not use Prompt and Project Data to train our or third-party AI models unless, before you use the applicable model or feature, we clearly state that the data may be used for AI training. A label or notice such as May use data for AI training means that Prompt and Project Data submitted through that model or feature may be retained and used by Company or the applicable provider to train, evaluate, and improve AI models and products. We may also share, license, or sell that data, including agent traces, to third parties, such as researchers and companies, who may use it for AI training, research, and other commercial purposes. A general notice that data is collected or stored does not by itself permit these uses.
## Phone and Identity Verification
To keep free access to one account per person, we may ask you to verify a phone number by text message, using an SMS provider acting on our behalf, and we check whether that number is already used by another account.
You may be able to verify your identity from your account settings, and when we need to confirm that an account belongs to a unique person (for example, an account that shares a device with another account, or that shows signs of abuse) we may require it, including before the account can keep using free features. Verification is performed by our processor Didit (Didit Identity S.L.). With your consent, Didit processes a photo of your government ID and a short selfie, including biometric data (a template of your facial geometry), and information about the network you connect from, to check that the document is genuine, that the selfie is a live capture that matches it, and whether the same face or document was already used for another Freebuff account.
We keep the result, the document's issuing country and type, a keyed hash of the document number, Didit's risk signals, and a record of the terms you accepted, but not your photos, selfie, name, or date of birth. We use these records to detect an identity used on more than one account or a false identity, which leads to the suspension of the accounts involved. Didit keeps its copy only for the retention period configured for our account, and biometric data is never retained longer than necessary for this purpose and in any case no longer than three years after your last interaction with us. When you delete your account, we ask Didit to erase your verification, except where the account was suspended for abuse.
## Device Identification
To detect duplicate, shared, and automated accounts, we use Fingerprint (FingerprintJS, Inc.), a device-identification provider acting as our service provider. When you sign up on our website, Fingerprint's script collects technical attributes of your browser and device and returns a visitor identifier with a confidence score and signals such as whether a VPN, proxy, virtual machine, browser tampering, developer tools, incognito mode, or automation (a bot) was detected. Our CLI and desktop app send installation and machine identifiers, and our website sets a device cookie.
We store keyed hashes of these identifiers (not the raw values), with those signals and the networks an account uses, and compare them across accounts to find accounts that share a device or network. We use the results to require verification, limit access, or suspend accounts that break our Terms. We keep these records while the account exists and as long as needed to prevent abuse.

## Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing, maintaining, developing, improving, evaluating, securing, and supporting Service; advertising; enforcing our Terms; complying with law; and resolving disputes.
Depending on the data and context, information may be deleted, deidentified, aggregated, or retained longer when reasonably needed for legitimate business, security, legal, dispute-resolution, or clearly disclosed AI-training purposes. Data submitted through a model or feature labeled for AI training may be retained for the permitted uses above and may not be removable after it is deidentified, incorporated into a model, or provided to a third party.
Saved projects and conversation history generally remain associated with your account until we complete an applicable deletion request, subject to the exceptions above.
Neither we nor our Codebase Evaluation partners retain raw source code or agent traces solely for Codebase Evaluation after we complete an account-deletion request or a verified privacy request to delete that data. Disconnecting GitHub, removing Freebuff’s repository access, or selecting “Delete project” in Freebuff Cloud do not by themselves delete data already collected.
When you delete your account, we keep keyed hashes of its identifiers, such as email address, phone number, identity document, and device identifiers, with its suspension, limit, and reward status, so that deleting an account does not reset a suspension, limit, or reward.
Deleted information may remain in backups, and deidentified or aggregated data, derived interests, derived Codebase Evaluation results that do not contain raw source code or agent traces, permitted training data, and content others retain through a sharing or publishing feature may remain after deletion. Where required, we instruct other service providers to delete information as well.
