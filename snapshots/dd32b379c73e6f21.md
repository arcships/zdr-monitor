## Pricing FAQ
How is agent usage billed?[+]
Warp shows agent usage in dollars. Build includes $20 of agent usage each month, Business includes $20 per seat each month, and Max includes $200 each month. These amounts cover usage in the Warp app, Warp Agent CLI, and Oz cloud agents.
The cost of an action depends on the model, the size of the task, the context and codebase involved, and whether the agent runs locally or in the cloud. Cloud and managed-agent runs can also include platform and compute costs.
Is the Warp Agent CLI included in my plan?[+]
Yes. The Warp Agent CLI is included with every Warp plan on macOS and Linux. Sign in with the same Warp account to use your plan, models, and synced conversations.
CLI requests draw on the same included or purchased agent usage as the Warp app. Free users can purchase additional usage or add an OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google API key or SuperGrok subscription with `/api-keys`. Warp's Auto models use Warp-provided inference.
The CLI footer shows total conversation usage. Run `/cost` to see the cost of each response.
Does Warp have a SOC 2 Type II attestation?[+]
Yes, Warp has obtained a SOC 2 Type II attestation from an accredited third party. Visit Warp's Trust Center to request the report.
Which AI models can I use in Warp?[+]
Paid plans include frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI, plus top open-source models like GLM 5.2, Kimi K3, and DeepSeek V4 hosted on Fireworks AI in the US. Model access is the same in the Warp app and the Warp Agent CLI.
You can pick a model per prompt, or let Warp's Auto routing choose for you — including an open-weights option that routes between the best open-source models for lower cost and faster speed.
On the Free plan, you can bring your own inference instead: use your own API keys, connect your SuperGrok subscription, or connect a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
See the full list of supported models in our docs.
Can I bring my own AI inference?[+]
Yes. Warp supports several ways to use your own AI inference with Warp Agent:
- **API keys:** Connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google API key.
- **SuperGrok subscription:** Sign in with your xAI account to run Grok models through your existing SuperGrok subscription — no API key needed.
- **Custom inference endpoints:** Connect an OpenAI-compatible provider or gateway, such as OpenRouter, LiteLLM, z.ai, or an internal gateway.
- **Inference in your cloud (BYOLLM):** On Enterprise, run inference through models hosted in your own cloud, such as AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI.
- **Team-managed API keys and endpoints:** On Enterprise, admins centrally configure API keys and endpoints for their team, including for cloud agents.
In the Warp Agent CLI, add API keys or a SuperGrok subscription with `/api-keys`. Custom inference endpoints are available only in the Warp app.
Self-serve API keys, SuperGrok subscriptions, and custom inference endpoints are available to individual users and organizations with 10 or fewer employees, subject to Warp's Terms of Service; larger organizations require a Warp Business or Enterprise plan. Contact us if you have questions about the right setup for your team.
With BYOLLM, admins configure inference centrally for the whole team and no API keys are stored. Inference runs and is billed in your cloud account, not through Warp. AWS Bedrock and Google Vertex AI are supported today, with Azure AI Foundry coming soon. Learn more about Bring Your Own LLM in our docs.
Warp's Zero Data Retention agreements apply to Warp-provided inference. When you bring your own inference, requests are sent directly to your provider under your own account and terms.
What's the difference between bringing my own API key (BYOK) and bringing my own LLM (BYOLLM)?[+]
**Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK)** is set up by each user: add your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google API key (stored on your device) and Warp routes requests to the provider with your key. BYOK is available to individual users and organizations with 10 or fewer employees, subject to Warp's Terms of Service; larger organizations need a Business or Enterprise plan.
**Bring Your Own LLM (BYOLLM)** is an Enterprise feature that admins configure for the whole team: inference runs through models hosted in your own cloud account, such as AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI, with no stored API keys and costs billed to your cloud account.
Enterprise admins can also configure **team-managed API keys and endpoints**: shared provider keys and inference endpoints that work for the whole team, including cloud agents.
Contact us if you're not sure which setup fits your organization.
Does Warp have Zero Data Retention agreements with LLM providers?[+]
Yes. Warp has Zero Data Retention agreements with supported LLM providers, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, and Fireworks AI.
For supported models, these agreements prevent providers from training on customer-generated data processed through Warp, and require prompts and outputs to be deleted after the response is generated within a fixed period of time.
Zero Data Retention applies to Warp-provided inference only. If you use your own API key, SuperGrok subscription, custom endpoint, or LLM, requests are sent directly to your provider under your own account and terms.
How can I enable Zero Data Retention in Warp?[+]
Zero Data Retention (ZDR) can be enabled in two ways:
- **Individual level:** Any user can enable full ZDR for their own account by disabling **Help Improve Warp** in `Settings > Privacy`.
- **Organization-wide:** On the Business and Enterprise plans, admins can enforce ZDR for all members by default via the admin panel, ensuring team-wide compliance without relying on individual settings.
If you'd like to request organization-wide ZDR, please contact your Warp sales representative at sales@warp.dev or submit a request via our Contact Sales form.
Should I subscribe to the Build plan, Max plan, or the Business plan?[+]
Build is a good fit for individual developers or small teams that want flexible access to Warp AI. It includes $20 of usage at API rates each month, optional add-on usage, full Warp Agent access, and higher codebase indexing limits.
Max is best for heavy AI users who consistently need more capacity. It includes $200 of usage each month — 10× Build.
Business is best for teams that need SSO, team usage metrics, admin-configurable data controls, and centralized spend management. It supports up to 25 team members and includes $20 of usage per seat each month.
For larger organizations needing unlimited seats, advanced governance, custom usage terms, or self-hosted cloud agents, contact us about Enterprise.
How can I subscribe to a Warp Business or Enterprise plan?[+]
You can subscribe to the Business plan directly from our upgrade page or via your in-app `Settings > Billing & usage` page.
For larger engineering organizations or companies that need advanced compliance, governance, custom usage terms, or dedicated support, Warp also offers an Enterprise plan with custom pricing and deployment options. To learn more, contact us at sales@warp.dev or submit a request.
Can I purchase additional agent usage on any plan?[+]
Yes. Free, Build, Max, and Business users can purchase additional agent usage.
Add-on usage is shared across your team, rolls over month to month, and remains valid for 12 months. Paid-plan admins can set team spend caps and auto-reload. Build, Max, and Business also include monthly agent usage.
When does included AI usage reset?[+]
Included monthly usage resets every 30 days from your subscription or renewal date. You can view your remaining balance under `Settings > Billing & usage`.
If you run out before the next reset, you can purchase additional agent usage to keep using AI features without interruption. On eligible plans, admins can also configure auto-reload and team-wide spend caps.
How does additional agent usage work for teams?[+]
Included monthly usage is assigned to each paid seat and resets every 30 days from the team's subscription or renewal date. Purchased add-on usage is pooled across the team: when any member purchases more, the whole team shares the balance.
Team admins can set a team-wide spend cap and choose whether members purchase add-on usage manually or use auto-reload. With auto-reload on, Warp purchases the selected add-on when the team's balance falls below the threshold, as long as the team remains under its spend cap.
Are there any Warp discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams?[+]
At this time, Warp does not offer discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams. We recommend Warp's Free plan, which includes the core terminal features plus support for bringing your own AI inference.
What payment options are available?[+]
Warp uses Stripe for payment processing and the only available payment method is by credit card.
Warp cannot currently accept payment by ACH, cash, check, money order, or cryptocurrency.
