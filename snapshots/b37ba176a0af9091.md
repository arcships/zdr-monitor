1. Definitions
“Adapter” means an object that is designed to be compatible with the designated Model and intended to adjust the Model Output in accordance with the algorithm contained within the Adapter. “Authorized User” means any End User who has been explicitly designated by Customer and granted specific administrative rights and permissions to manage the Token Factory Service. “Customer AI Content” means Customer’s Inputs, Outputs, Adapters, Fine-Tuned Models, Distilled Models, Datasets, Models and smaller Models for Speculative Decoding. For the avoidance of doubt, Customer AI Content is considered Customer Data for purposes of the Agreement (including data deletion, Customer obligations, and indemnification), unless expressly stated otherwise in these Token Factory Terms or an applicable Order Form. “Customer Product” means any product, service, application, or system developed, managed, or distributed by Customer that utilizes or integrates with the Token Factory Service. “Dataset” means any collection of data that is used for training, validating, fine-tuning Models or other purposes, and includes both datasets that are provided by Customer and uploaded to the Platform, as well as those datasets that are created or generated by Customer on the Platform itself. “Distillation” means the process of training a smaller Model to replicate the behavior of a larger Model while maintaining similar performance characteristics. “Distilled Model” means the resulting Model created through Distillation. “Dedicated Endpoint” means configurable deployment of a supported Model with dedicated capacity exclusively for Customer’s use, which Customer may create and manage through the Platform. “End User” means any individual who, subject to compliance with the Agreement (including these Token Factory Terms): (a) accesses or uses the Token Factory Service under Customer’s Account; or (b) uses a Customer Product that utilizes or integrates with the Token Factory Service. “EU AI Act” means Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act) as amended. “EULA” means a license agreement, an acceptable use policy, and/or any other similar document published by a Model’s provider and governing the use of the Model plus additional rules applicable to a Model’s usage indicated in Models’ cards. “Fine-Tuned Model” means a Model that has undergone Fine-Tuning. “Fine-Tuning” means the process of adapting Model to enhance its performance on specific tasks or Datasets. “Input” means any data, text, image, audio, video query, or other information Customer submits through the Platform to prompt Models. “Model” means any computational algorithm, system, or framework developed to analyze data and learn patterns for performing specific tasks, including but not limited to prediction, classification, generation, or decision-making. “Operations” means actions performed by Token Factory Service to manage, retrieve, create, or modify cloud resources. “Output” means any text information generated by the Models in response to the Input provided by Customer through the Platform. “Speculative Decoding” means an inference-time method that speeds up Output generation by using a smaller, specific-purpose (“draft”) Model to propose multiple tokens, which are then verified or rejected by a larger (“target”) Model. Speculative decoding reduces the number of steps the target Model must compute while preserving its Output quality and significantly increases the speed of the Services while maintaining the quality of the Model’s responses. “Token Factory API/SDK” means an API, software development kit, or other similar set of protocols and tools that enables End Users to send Input to Models and receive Output. “Token Factory API/SDK Keys” means unique identifiers provided by Nebius to Customer to authenticate and authorize their access to Token API/SDK(s).
