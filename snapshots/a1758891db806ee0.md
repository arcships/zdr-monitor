## 6. DATA RETENTION
We will retain personal data in Section 3 for as long as it is necessary for the fulfilment of the purpose(s) as described in this Module and as required or authorized by applicable laws, to resolve disputes and to enforce our terms and conditions, other applicable terms of service, and our policies.
The actual retention period can vary for different data categories, depending on the context of your interactions with us, your configuration set up and your use of the Features (e.g., if you request or set a longer or shorter retention period).
For Configuration Data and Diagnostic and Usage Data, we generally retain information for up to 7 days after your task request in connection with the Features to provide you the Features and for troubleshooting purposes, but may process or retain such data for a longer period (for example as long as you use the Features) for billing purposes. For Customer Content and Service Generated Content, we generally retain information for up to 7 days after your task request in connection with the Feature.
If you have any questions or require additional information relating to the above (including where you require further or more specific information on how long we store your personal data), please refer to our Contact Information section in our Privacy Policy.

You are responsible for all content submitted to the Features, including any personal data relating to third parties. You must ensure that you have all rights, consents, and other lawful basis necessary under applicable laws to collect, use, disclose, and process such information in connection with your use of the Features.
In addition, these Features are not intended for the processing of sensitive data. Please ensure that the Features are not used to transfer or otherwise process any sensitive data entered you, including (but not limited to) political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs relating to you or other individuals.
