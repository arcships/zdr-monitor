Realized savings depend on your prompts, traffic patterns, and cache hit rate, which are determined by your workload. We do not guarantee any cache hit rate, retention outcome, or level of savings, and any savings figures shown in the control panel or Documentation are estimates only.
9.3 Retention; Ephemerality; No Backup. Cache entries are ephemeral and subject to eviction and retention windows described in the Documentation, including any in-memory and persistent (tiered-storage) retention behavior, which we may change. Prompt Caching is not a storage or backup feature, and Section 4.6 of the TOS applies. We do not guarantee that any cache entry will persist or be available for any period.

Beyond our isolation controls described in the Documentation, we do not warrant that response-timing or similar characteristics cannot be observed.
9.5 Opt-Out.You may disable Prompt Caching as described in the Documentation and request that applicable cache entries be flushed. Disabling Prompt Caching means eligible tokens will be billed at standard rates.
9.6 Routing and Processing. To provide Prompt Caching, we may route requests to, and store or process cache entries on, infrastructure and storage tiers we select, consistent with Section 4.7 of the TOS.
