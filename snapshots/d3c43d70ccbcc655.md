### Node.js, PHP, and eMudhra Security Advisories
Vulnerabilities
In October 2026, Vercel published three advisories affecting runtimes and certificates: a compromised eMudhra certificate authority (October 17), a Node.js Brotli vulnerability (October 21), and the php2shell PHP vulnerabilities (October 21). Where needed, Vercel has applied protections to the platform.
What to do:
* Projects on Vercel: Redeploy to get the patched Node.js and Vercel's added protections. Projects on Node.js 20.x must switch to 22.x or 24.x and redeploy.
* PHP, anywhere: Upgrade to 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 or 8.5.12.
* Your own runtimes and servers: Upgrade Node.js to 22.23.4, 24.22.1 or 26.12.1. Update certificate bundles, such as certifi 2026.10.19 or later, and rebuild on updated container base images.
