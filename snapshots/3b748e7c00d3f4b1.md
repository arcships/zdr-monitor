# Databricks Designated Services
This article describes Databricks Designated Services, which are features in Databricks that use Databricks Geos to manage data residency when processing customer content.
important
If a Designated Service is available in your workspace's Geo, then your content is processed in that Geo. Some Designated Services are not natively available in all Geos, and admins might need to enable cross-Geo processing before these Designated Services can be used. If you enable cross-Geo processing on your workspace, the Designated Service can process your content in other Geos. Your data is not stored outside of your workspace's Geo regardless of this setting, with one exception: Genie Search stores a copy of your connected sources' content and permissions in the Geo an account admin selects during setup, which can differ from the Geos of some of your workspaces. See Set up Genie Search.

¶ For AI Runtime, some GPU types require cross-Geo processing. For AWS workspaces in Asia, Australia & New Zealand, Europe, and India, cross-Geo GPU workloads run in the Americas Geo.
** For Genie Code CLI, workspaces in Brazil, Canada, and the United Kingdom require cross-Geo processing. With cross-Geo processing disabled, workspaces in Australia and New Zealand have fewer models and a smaller context window.
For AI assistive features in US and EU regions (regardless of cross-Geo processing setting), workspaces in the Americas use a US-based model, and workspaces in Europe use a Europe-based model.
