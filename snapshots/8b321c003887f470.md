# Data Processing
Data Lab works with several types of data, such as inference logs (chat completions), uploaded datasets, batch inference outputs, and fine-tuning results. To ensure full transparency, this section explains how your data is processed, your level of control, and where processing takes place.
**How We Handle Your Data**
For all data you submit or generate through Data Lab, including:
- Inference logs - inputs and outputs (unless you enable Zero Data Retention)
- Uploaded datasets
- Filtered datasets
- Batch inference outputs
- Fine-tuning results
Nebius Token Factory acts as a **Data Processor** under the terms of our Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
**Processing Location**
All Data Lab processing activities, including handling of logs, datasets, batch outputs, and training data, are performed exclusively in the EU-North1 (Finland) data center.
**Future Changes**
If hosting locations change, we commit to notifying you in advance so you can review compliance implications and adjust your workflows if needed.
