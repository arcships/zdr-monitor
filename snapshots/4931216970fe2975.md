# Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 3, 2026
## 1. Introduction
Amp Frontier Corporation (“Amp,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides AI coding tools, our website at ampcode.com, our documentation and related services (together, the “Services”). This privacy policy (this “Privacy Policy”) explains what personal information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.
If you are a California resident, Section 9 (Your California Privacy Rights) describes your rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended (the “CCPA”). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in our Terms of Service.
## 2. Summary of Key Points
2.1 We do not sell your personal information for money.
2.2 We do share limited personal information with Google, Meta, LinkedIn, X and Reddit through advertising pixels on our website and by reporting conversion events to them from our servers, so we can measure our ads and show Amp ads to you on other sites. Under California law this is “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising.
2.3 You can opt out of this sharing at any time through the “Your Privacy Choices” link, which visitors outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see in the footer of every page of our website, including our documentation, or by turning on Global Privacy Control (“GPC”) in your browser. We honor GPC automatically. Opting out stops the advertising pixels; GPC also stops our own analytics cookies.
2.4 Our own website analytics are first-party only and are not shared with advertising companies.
2.5 For visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and visitors whose location we cannot determine, we do not use analytics or advertising cookies or tags on our website. We keep only an anonymous count of page loads with the country, with no cookie or ID.
2.6 You can ask to know, delete or correct your personal information by emailing privacy@ampcode.com.

## 4. Cookies, Pixels and Similar Technologies
### 4.1 First-party website analytics
On our public website (for example, our home, marketing, documentation, pricing and signup pages), we measure how visitors use the site, for example the pages they view, how they move between pages, and the buttons and links they click. We record the page path, the external referring site without query strings, UTM campaign tags, your country, which we derive from your IP address without storing it, the label and destination of a button or link you click, and technical details such as your browser window width. On pages whose address contains a private identifier, such as shared thread and gift code pages, we do not record that identifier. Our own analytics do not record your IP address, your browser user agent, anything you type into forms, or other URL parameters. This data is stored on our own servers and in our cloud data warehouse, operated by our cloud provider as our service provider, and is not sent to any analytics or advertising company. Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland these analytics run by default; a GPC signal turns them off (see Section 4.4).
Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, if you create an Amp account, we link your analytics visitor ID to your new account so we can understand which pages led to signup, and if you use our public website while signed in, we record that activity with your account so we can understand how customers use our website and documentation. Activity linked to your account, including your earlier website activity, becomes part of your account information and is covered by your rights to know and delete. If you delete your account, we delete this linked activity.
**Signup attribution.** When you visit our website without being signed in, we may also set first-party cookies that remember how you arrived at our website, such as the referring site and campaign tags. If you create an account, we save this information with your account so we can see which campaigns lead to signups, and we delete it when you delete your account. We do not set these cookies in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, or if your browser sends a GPC signal.
### 4.2 Advertising pixels
We use advertising pixels and tags from Google (the Google tag, for Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing), Meta (the Meta Pixel), LinkedIn (the LinkedIn Insight Tag), X (the X Pixel) and Reddit (the Reddit Pixel). When you visit our website, these tools may collect your IP address, browser and device information, cookie and advertising IDs, the pages you view, and actions you take such as signing up. Google, Meta, LinkedIn, X and Reddit may combine this information with other information they hold about you, and use it to measure our ads and to show you Amp ads on their platforms and other websites. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies:
- Google: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Meta: www.facebook.com/privacy/policy
- LinkedIn: www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy
- X: x.com/en/privacy
- Reddit: www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy
**Conversion events reported from our servers.** We also report conversions to these partners directly from our servers (the partners call this enhanced conversions, advanced matching or a conversions API) when you take actions such as signing up, first using Amp, making a purchase (including the amount), creating a workspace or contacting sales. Some of these events happen while you are signed in to the Services. With each event we may send a hashed version of your email address, ad click identifiers, your IP address, browser information and the value of partner cookies such as Meta’s _fbp. We do not send content you submit to the Services, such as code, prompts or thread content, or anything you type into forms other than the sales inquiry itself, and we do not report events for a browser or account that has opted out or sent a GPC signal.
**Where these tags run.** We load advertising pixels on our public website, for example our home, pricing and documentation pages, for visitors who are not signed in. We do not load them inside the signed-in Services or on websites we host for enterprise customers under their own domain names.
Our advertising partners may also set or read cookies on their own domains under their own policies, for example Meta’s fr cookie, LinkedIn’s bcookie, lidc and UserMatchHistory cookies, and X’s personalization_id, muc_ads and guest_id cookies. Cookie names and lifetimes set by our partners are controlled by them and may change; the table below reflects their published information at the date of this policy.

### 4.4 How your choices affect these technologies
4.4.1 **Visitors outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland.** Our first-party analytics and advertising pixels run by default unless you opt out as described below, and we link your website activity to your account when you sign up or use our website while signed in. If you opt out through “Your Privacy Choices” or your browser sends a GPC signal, we stop loading advertising pixels in that browser (they do not load at all) and stop reporting conversion events from our servers for that browser and, if you are signed in, for your account, so we no longer use your information for sharing. Opting out through “Your Privacy Choices” does not turn off our own first-party analytics, which we never share with advertising partners. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we also do not set our own analytics cookies, and we record only anonymous page counts. We remember an opt-out for at least 12 months.
4.4.2 **EEA, UK and Switzerland visitors.** We do not use analytics or advertising cookies or tags on our website for you, whether or not you are signed in. We do not set analytics or advertising cookies, load advertising pixels, keep ad click IDs or link your website activity to your account, and if you create an account, we do not report your sign-up or later purchases to advertising partners from our servers. We keep only an anonymous count of page loads (the page, referring site and country, with no cookie or ID). If your browser still has our own analytics, signup attribution or ad click ID cookies from an earlier visit, we delete them on your next visit. Cookies that advertising partners set during an earlier visit, such as _fbp and _gcl_au, are not under our control and expire on their own. These rules also apply if we cannot tell which country you are in.
4.4.3 **Your Privacy Choices.** Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can change your choice at any time through the “Your Privacy Choices” link in the footer of every page of our website, including our documentation.
4.4.4 **Browser and platform controls.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser and manage ad preferences in your account settings with each advertising partner. These controls are separate from, and do not replace, your CCPA opt-out.
4.4.5 **Do Not Track.** Do Not Track has no common standard, so we do not respond to it. We do honor GPC as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Sale and Sharing of Personal Information
We do not sell personal information for money. This includes the hashed email addresses and conversion events we report to these partners from our servers, described in Section 4.2. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.
### 7.1 Your right to opt out of sale and sharing
You have the right to direct us not to sell or share your personal information. You can opt out in either of these ways:
- **“Your Privacy Choices.”** Click the “Your Privacy Choices” link in our website footer and turn off sale and sharing. You do not need an account, and we will not ask you to verify your identity.
- **Global Privacy Control.** Turn on Global Privacy Control in a browser or extension that supports it. Visit globalprivacycontrol.org for supported browsers and extensions.
### 7.2 How we process Global Privacy Control
We process opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, in a frictionless manner. When we detect a GPC signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of sale and sharing for that browser or device and for any profile we associate with it, including pseudonymous profiles. If you are signed in, we also apply it to your account. An opt-out saved on your account stays in place until you choose to opt back in. We do not charge you, change your experience of the Services, or show you a pop-up in response to the signal. Our website displays whether your opt-out has been honored, for example “Opt-Out Request Honored.”
We stop sharing as soon as feasibly possible, and no later than 15 business days after we receive your request. We will wait at least 12 months before asking you to opt back in.

## 9. Your California Privacy Rights
If you are a California resident, you have the following rights, subject to exceptions in the CCPA:
- **Right to know and access.** You may ask us to disclose the categories of personal information we collected about you, the categories of sources, our business or commercial purposes, the categories of third parties to whom we disclosed or shared it, and the specific pieces of personal information we hold about you.
- **Right to delete.** You may ask us to delete personal information we collected from you. You can also delete your account at any time in your account settings. When we delete your information, we will also direct our service providers and the third parties with whom we shared it to delete it, unless that proves impossible or involves disproportionate effort.
- **Right to correct.** You may ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
- **Right to opt out of sale or sharing.** You may opt out at any time as described in Section 7.1.
- **Right to limit use of sensitive personal information.** We use sensitive personal information only for permitted purposes, so this right does not apply to our practices.
- **Right to non-discrimination.** We will not deny you service, charge you a different price or provide a different quality of service because you exercised your rights.
### 9.1 How to submit a request to know, delete or correct
Email us at privacy@ampcode.com with the subject line “California Privacy Request” and tell us which right you want to exercise. You may also delete your account directly in your account settings.
### 9.2 Verification
We verify requests to know, delete and correct by matching information you provide with information we already hold. Usually we ask you to confirm control of the email address on your account. We will not ask for more information than we need. We do not require verification for opt-out requests or GPC signals.
### 9.3 Authorized agents
You may use an authorized agent to submit a request. We may require the agent to provide your signed permission and may ask you to verify your identity directly with us. This requirement does not apply to opt-out preference signals.
### 9.4 Response timing
We will confirm receipt of a request to know, delete or correct within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days. If we need more time, up to 45 additional days, we will tell you why.
