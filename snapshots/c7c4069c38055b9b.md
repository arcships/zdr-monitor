# Xiaomi MiMo Privacy Policy
Our Privacy Policy was published and became effective as of 25 June 2026. Please take some time to read through our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions, please contact us.

Xiaomi MiMo is an API open platform service and a suite of tools, including Xiaomi MiMo Code, provided to you by Beijing Xiaomi Mobile Software Co., Ltd. and its affiliates (hereinafter collectively referred to as "Xiaomi" or "we"), powered by generative artificial intelligence model technology.
We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy serves as a specific statement and commitment regarding privacy for Xiaomi MiMo, and constitutes an important component of the Xiaomi Privacy Policy. In the event of any inconsistency between this Privacy Policy and the Xiaomi Privacy Policy in terms of specific agreements involving Xiaomi MiMo, the terms of this Privacy Policy shall take precedence. Matters not expressly provided for under this Privacy Policy shall be governed by the Xiaomi Privacy Policy. For detailed provisions regarding the protection of minors' information, security measures, and cross-border data transfer, please refer to the Xiaomi Privacy Policy .
This Privacy Policy has been developed with you in mind. It is of utmost importance to us that you fully understand our practices regarding the collection and use of your personal information and that you retain control over the information you provide to us. This Private Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, process, and store the information you provide to us when using our Xiaomi MiMo services. For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "Personal Information" refers to any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that is recorded electronically or by other means, excluding information that has been anonymised. We will strictly comply with this Privacy Policy in our use of such information.
Ultimately, we are committed to providing you with the best possible experience. If you have any questions regarding our personal information processing practices as described in this Privacy Policy, please contact us by visiting https://privacy.mi.com/support so that we may address your specific needs.
## I. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND HOW WE USE IT
We shall process your personal information in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, and necessity, and for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, as we collect and use the information that you voluntarily provide or that is generated during your use of our products or services. If we intend to use your personal information for any purpose not specified in this Privacy Policy, or for any purpose other than that for which it was originally collected, we shall notify you in a reasonable manner and obtain your consent again prior to such use.
Please note that we have highlighted sensitive personal information in bold to draw your attention to it; we encourage you to pay close attention to these sections as you read this Privacy Policy. Before providing us with any sensitive personal information, you have carefully considered the implications and hereby confirm that such provision is appropriate. You further consent to your sensitive personal information being processed for the purposes and in the manner described in this Privacy Policy.
(i) Circumstances where you must authorise us to collect and use your personal information We collect your personal information for the purpose of providing you with our products and/or services, and to ensure our compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and other normative documents. You have the right to choose whether or not to provide such information; however, in most cases, if you choose not to provide it, we may be unable to provide you with the relevant services or respond to your enquiries. These functions include:
1. Account registration and login
You are required to log in to your Xiaomi Account to access the features of Xiaomi MiMo. To this end, we will collect your Xiaomi Account ID and phone number to generate an API Key. This API Key is used to verify your identity and display your account information when providing API services.
2. Xiaomi MiMo service
We require identity verification when you use or top up the paid services available on the Xiaomi MiMo open platform.
When you apply for individual identity verification, we will collect your full name, ID type, ID number, contact information (such as phone number), and industry details to verify your identity and complete the real-name authentication process.
When you apply for corporate identity verification, we will collect the administrator's name and contact information of your company to complete the real-name authentication process. To provide you with invoice issuance services, we will collect your email address and phone number to send you the completed invoices. To provide you with prepaid API services, we will collect your top-up information and transaction records generated during your use of these services.
To provide you with the Xiaomi MiMo API service and Xiaomi MiMo Code service, we will collect the text, image, video, and audio information you provide to analyse and execute relevant instructions based on your selected model, and to generate the output content. Additionally, we will collect your IP information to enhance service security, such as determining the general region of your device when accessing our services and protecting your account by detecting abnormal login activities.
Without your prior consent, Xiaomi will not use the text content provided by you for model training or any other purposes.
3. Voice mimicry
When you use the voice mimicry feature, we will collect the audio files and the voiceprint information contained therein to generate content based on the voice characteristics from the audio. You shall ensure that you have obtained legal, sufficient, and explicit consent from the data subjects for the use of their personal information (where sensitive personal information is involved, separate consent shall be obtained as required). You shall not upload any audio content that is illegal, non-compliant, or infringes upon the rights and interests of any third party. You authorise Xiaomi MiMo to process the information you upload on your behalf to provide the services, and no further separate authorisation shall be required for such purpose. 4. Issue feedback and notifications
We will collect your name (or form of address), contact number/email address, the description of your reported issue, and any related images to provide you with issue feedback services.
5. Push notifications
You acknowledge and agree that, in order to provide you with balance alerts, notifications, identity authentication, security verification, and invoice issuance services, we may send you notifications via the contact information you have provided (e.g., contact number, email address, etc.). When you participate in relevant promotional activities within the platform, we will collect your email address to send notifications, distribute rewards, and provide relevant follow-up services.
(ii) How we use cookies and similar technologies
To ensure the normal and efficient operation of Xiaomi MiMo and to provide you with a simple, easy-to-use, and personalised online experience, we may store small data files known as Cookie on your terminal device and/or system. We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:
1. To remember your identity. For example, cookies and similar technologies help us identify you as our registered user, save the information you have provided to us, or assist in determining whether your login status is normal and whether there are any anomalies in your account.
2. To enable certain enhanced features on our website, such as remembering your language preferences.
3. To analyse your use of our products and/or services. We will collect information regarding your browsing activities to better understand how you navigate and interact with our website. The information collected by these cookies is used to determine:
-The performance of our website. For example, page visit counts, session duration, feature usage and click counts, site events, overall usage metrics, performance data, and any specific errors encountered on the website.
-Information about the device used to access our website. For example, browser, operating system, device model, etc.
We will not use cookies for any purposes other than those described in this Privacy Policy.
You may manage or delete the saved cookies through your browser settings. You have the right to accept or decline cookies. You may modify your browser settings to reject them at your preference. However, this may prevent you from fully experiencing certain convenient and secure features of our products and/or services.
When you browse the Xiaomi MiMo open platform pages whilst not logged in, we will collect essential cookie information necessary to provide the corresponding products and/or services and to facilitate your browsing experience.

2. Where it relates to public safety, public health, or significant public interests;
3. Where it relates to criminal investigations, prosecutions, trials, or the execution of judgments;
4. Where it is necessary to protect the life, property, or other major legitimate rights and interests of the personal information subject or other individuals, but it is difficult to obtain the consent of the subject;

6. Where personal information is collected from legally disclosed information, such as through lawful news reports, government information disclosure channels, etc.;
7. Where it is necessary for entering into or performing a contract as requested by you;
8. Where it is necessary to maintain the safe and stable operation of the products and/or services provided, such as for the detection and resolution of faults in the products and/or services;

(iv) Non-personal information
We may also collect other information that cannot be used to identify any specific individual (i.e., information that does not constitute personal information). We are committed to ensuring the security of your personal information. Similarly, we maintain dedicated access control measures for cloud-based data storage. Therefore, we cannot guarantee the security or integrity of any personal information during its transmission to or from us via the internet.
We will handle any personal information breaches in accordance with applicable data protection regulations and, where required, promptly notify the relevant supervisory authorities and affected data subjects of the breach.

Although we will take all legally required measures to protect your personal information, you should be aware that the use of the internet is not entirely secure. Where it is difficult to notify each individual data subject, we will issue a public announcement in a reasonable and effective manner. In addition, we will report the progress and outcome of the handling of personal information security incidents to the regulatory authorities in accordance with their requirements.

We retain your personal information for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected as described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable laws and regulations. Upon completion of the purposes for which your personal information was collected, or after we confirm your request for deletion or account cancellation, or upon the termination of our operation of the corresponding products or services, we will cease retention of your personal information and proceed to delete or anonymise it.
